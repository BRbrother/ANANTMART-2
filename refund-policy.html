Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear and transparent cancellation and refund process.

At Gram Mart, we aim to provide a smooth and transparent shopping experience. This policy explains how customers can cancel eligible orders, request refunds and report product issues.

Effective Date: 1 November 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an eligible order through the My Orders section of the Gram Mart website before the order reaches Out for Delivery status.

  • Cancellation is subject to the current order status.
  • Customers should use the available cancellation option in My Orders.
  • Once an order reaches Out for Delivery status, normal customer cancellation is unavailable.
  • Delivered or already Cancelled orders cannot be cancelled again.

If the cancellation option is unavailable, customers can contact our support team for assistance.

2. Cancellation by Gram Mart

Gram Mart may cancel an order when it cannot reasonably be processed or delivered.

  • Ordered product is unavailable.
  • Delivery address is incorrect, incomplete or outside our active delivery area.
  • Payment cannot be verified where online payment is required.
  • There is suspected fraudulent activity or misuse.
  • A technical or operational issue prevents order fulfilment.

Where an eligible prepaid order is cancelled by Gram Mart, the applicable refund process will be initiated.

3. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • An eligible prepaid order is successfully cancelled.
  • Gram Mart cancels a prepaid order and the payment was received.
  • A refund is approved after reviewing an incorrect, damaged or missing product complaint.
  • A duplicate or excess payment is verified.
  • Another refund situation is approved under applicable law or our customer support review.

Each refund request is subject to verification and applicable customer rights.

4. Prepaid Order Refund Process

For an eligible prepaid cancellation, customers may be asked to submit refund details through the refund option available in the My Orders section.

Depending on the available refund method, customers may provide:

  • UPI ID for UPI refund.
  • Account holder name for bank transfer.
  • Bank account number.
  • IFSC code.

Customers should carefully check the details before submission. Incorrect or incomplete details may delay the refund.

5. Refund Processing Time

Our target processing time is 5–7 business days after refund approval and successful verification of the required refund details.

Actual credit time may depend on the bank, UPI service provider and payment channel.

Important: The processing estimate begins after approval and verification. Incorrect details, bank delays or technical issues may require additional time.

6. Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

If a COD order is cancelled before delivery, no product payment refund is normally required because the customer has not paid for the order.

If any amount has already been collected or paid in relation to a COD order, the matter will be reviewed and handled according to the verified payment details and applicable policy.

7. Damaged, Incorrect or Missing Products

If you receive a damaged, incorrect or missing product, please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 24 hours of delivery.

Please provide:

  • Your order ID.
  • Name of the affected product.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Photographs or other supporting details, where available.

After reviewing the complaint, Gram Mart may offer an appropriate resolution such as replacement, refund or another suitable solution, subject to applicable law.

8. When Normal Cancellation Is Unavailable

The normal customer cancellation option is unavailable once an order reaches Out for Delivery or Delivered status.

If a customer has a genuine issue with an order that has already been dispatched or delivered, they should contact support. The issue will be reviewed separately according to its circumstances and applicable consumer rights.

9. Refund Safety & Verification

  • Submit accurate UPI or bank details.
  • Do not share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • Gram Mart does not need your banking password to process a refund.
  • Refund requests may be checked to prevent unauthorized claims or fraud.

10. Refund Request Status

Customers may use the My Orders section to access the refund submission option and view available refund information or request status, depending on the website features.

If you need clarification about a pending refund, contact customer support with your order ID.

Go to My Orders

11. Changes to This Policy

Gram Mart may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in operations, payment processes or applicable legal requirements.

Updated information will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
