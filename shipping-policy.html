Shipping & Delivery Policy

Everything you need to know about your Gram Mart delivery.

At Gram Mart, we aim to make grocery shopping convenient by delivering your orders to your doorstep. This policy explains how we process and deliver orders, where we currently operate and what customers can expect.

Effective Date: 1 November 2026

1. Delivery Location & Service Area

Gram Mart currently operates from its delivery centre in Aakeda Dunger, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

Our delivery service is available only in selected areas covered by our active delivery zone.

  • Delivery availability is checked using the address or location provided during checkout.
  • Customers must enter a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Orders outside the active delivery area may not be accepted.
  • Our service area may expand or change over time.

2. Order Processing

After an order is successfully placed, it is recorded in our system for processing.

  • Orders are reviewed and prepared subject to product availability.
  • Online payment orders may require payment verification.
  • COD orders are processed subject to service availability and confirmation where required.
  • We may contact customers if order details need clarification.

An order confirmation does not guarantee delivery if an item is unavailable or the delivery address is outside our service area.

3. Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery times may depend on order volume, product availability, distance, weather, traffic and delivery personnel availability.

  • Any delivery estimate shown during checkout is indicative.
  • Actual delivery time may vary due to operational conditions.
  • We will make reasonable efforts to deliver orders as promptly as possible.
  • Customers may contact support for assistance with delayed orders.

Important: Unless a specific delivery slot is displayed and confirmed at checkout, a delivery estimate should not be treated as a guaranteed delivery time.

4. Delivery Charges

Applicable delivery charges, if any, will be displayed during checkout before the customer confirms the order.

  • Delivery charges may depend on the order or service conditions.
  • Any applicable delivery fee will be included in the order total.
  • Promotional or free-delivery offers may be subject to separate conditions.

Customers should review the final payable amount before placing an order.

5. Available Payment Methods

Depending on availability, Gram Mart may accept:

  • Online UPI payment.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD).

For online orders, payment must be successfully verified. For COD orders, the customer must pay the applicable amount at the time of delivery.

6. Customer Availability at Delivery

Customers should ensure that someone is available at the provided delivery address to receive the order.

  • Please provide a working mobile number.
  • Keep your phone reachable around the expected delivery time.
  • Provide accurate landmarks or delivery instructions if necessary.
  • Ensure that the delivery location can be accessed safely.

7. Failed or Delayed Delivery

Delivery may be delayed or unsuccessful due to circumstances such as:

  • Customer unavailable at the delivery address.
  • Incorrect or incomplete address or contact information.
  • Traffic, weather or other local disruptions.
  • Unexpected operational or technical problems.
  • Temporary unavailability of the delivery service.

If delivery cannot be completed, our team may contact the customer to discuss the next appropriate step. Any cancellation or refund will be handled under the applicable policy.

8. Checking Your Order at Delivery

Customers are encouraged to check the delivered items at the time of receiving the order.

  • Check whether the delivered products match the order.
  • Check visible packaging damage or missing items.
  • Report incorrect, damaged or missing items to customer support as soon as possible, preferably within 24 hours.
  • Share your order ID and relevant photographs where available.

Any resolution will be reviewed according to the nature of the issue and applicable customer policies.

9. Cancellation & Refunds

Customers may request cancellation before an order reaches Out for Delivery status using the available My Orders option.

Eligible prepaid cancellations and approved refund requests will be handled according to our Cancellation & Refund Policy .

10. Delivery Support

If you have questions about delivery, an order delay or an unsuccessful delivery, please contact Gram Mart support.

Please keep your order ID ready so our team can assist you.

11. Changes to This Policy

Gram Mart may update this Shipping & Delivery Policy to reflect changes in delivery operations, service areas or applicable requirements.

Updated information will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

Need Help With Your Delivery?

Contact Gram Mart customer support for assistance with orders, delivery delays or service-area questions.
