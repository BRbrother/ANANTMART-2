Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust matter to us.

At Gram Mart, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the choices available to you when you use our website and grocery delivery services.

Effective Date: 1 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Gram Mart, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: Name and mobile number.
  • Account details: Email address and account-related information, if provided.
  • Delivery details: Address, landmark, postal code and delivery location coordinates where shared.
  • Order information: Products purchased, quantities, order ID, order status and order history.
  • Payment references: Payment method, transaction reference or UTR details needed to verify a payment or process a refund.
  • Support information: Messages and details you share when contacting our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your customer account.
  • To accept, confirm and process grocery orders.
  • To arrange delivery to your selected address.
  • To check whether your location is within our active delivery area.
  • To verify payments and handle eligible refunds.
  • To send important order updates and service notifications.
  • To respond to customer queries, complaints and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorized activity.
  • To improve our website and customer experience.

3. Location & Delivery Information

Gram Mart may request your delivery address or location information to determine whether your address is serviceable and to complete your order.

  • You may be asked to enter your address manually.
  • If you choose to share your device location, coordinates may be used to identify your delivery point.
  • Location information may be shared with the delivery personnel responsible for your order.
  • We do not need your location for unrelated purposes.

You can choose not to share device location, but you may need to provide a delivery address manually to place an order.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information when necessary to provide our services.

  • Delivery personnel: Customer name, phone number and delivery address required to complete delivery.
  • Payment service providers: Relevant payment or transaction information to process and verify payments.
  • Website and technology providers: Information necessary for website hosting, database, security and technical operations.
  • Legal authorities: Information when disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

However, no website, online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should also keep their account credentials safe.

Security Reminder: Gram Mart will never ask you to share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or banking password over WhatsApp, phone calls or messages.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Managing customer accounts and orders.
  • Handling payments, refunds and customer complaints.
  • Maintaining business and transaction records.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Resolving disputes and preventing misuse.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

7. Cookies & Website Technologies

Our website or its service providers may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, account sessions, cart operations and user experience.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain storage technologies may affect some website features.

8. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to:

  • Request information about the personal data associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permissible.
  • Raise a concern about how your information is being used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Some information may need to be retained to complete orders, process refunds, maintain required records or comply with law.

9. Children's Privacy

Gram Mart's website is intended for people who can legally place orders and enter into applicable transactions. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website may rely on third-party services for payments, hosting, maps, communications or other technical features.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage customers to review the policies of relevant third-party providers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

12. Contact & Privacy Complaints

If you have any questions, requests or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact Gram Mart through our customer support channel.
